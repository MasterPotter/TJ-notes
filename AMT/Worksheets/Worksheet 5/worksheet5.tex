    \item This problem concerns the use of the duplication identity of the Gamma function:
    \[\Gamma\left( \frac 1 2 + \varepsilon \right) = 2^{-2\varepsilon}\sqrt{\pi}\frac{\Gamma(1+2\varepsilon)}{\Gamma(1+\varepsilon)}\]
    to investigate the behavior of the Gamma function in the vicinity of $\frac 1 2$.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Plug in $\varepsilon = 4$ and work out the two sides of this identity separately.
        \\
        \[\Gamma\left(\frac{9}{2}\right) = \frac{\Gamma(9)\sqrt{\pi}}{256\Gamma(5)}\]
        \[\frac 7 2 \cdot \frac 5 2 \cdot \frac 3 2 \cdot \frac 1 2 \cdot \sqrt{\pi} = \frac{8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot\sqrt{\pi}}{256}\]
        \[\frac{7 \cdot 5 \cdot 3}{2^4}\sqrt{\pi} = \frac{7 \cdot 5 \cdot 3}{2^4}\sqrt{\pi} \hspace{3mm} \checkmark\]
        \item Thinking of $\varepsilon$ as a very small number, expand the right hand side of this identity. Keep up to $\varepsilon^2$. Note that this really isn't that bad if you combine all of the exponentials before expanding.
        \\
        \[2^{-2\varepsilon}\sqrt{\pi}\frac{\Gamma(1+2\varepsilon)}{\Gamma(1+\varepsilon)} = \sqrt{\pi}\exp(-2\varepsilon \ln 2 - \gamma \varepsilon + \frac{\pi^2}{4}\varepsilon^2) = \sqrt{\pi}\exp(-(\gamma + 2 \ln 2)\varepsilon + \frac{\pi^2}{4}\varepsilon^2)\]
        \[ \approx \boxed{\sqrt{\pi}\left[ 1 - (\gamma + 2 \ln 2)\varepsilon + \left( \frac{\pi^2}{4} + \frac{(\gamma + 2 \ln 2)^2}{2}\right)\varepsilon^2\right]}\]
        \item Your result from part (b) contains the first few terms in a Maclaurin expansion for $\Gamma(\frac 1 2 + \varepsilon)$. Use this fact to determine the exact values of $\Gamma'(\frac 1 2), \Gamma''(\frac 1 2)$.
        \\
        \[\Gamma\left(\frac{1}{2} + \varepsilon\right) = \Gamma\left( \frac 1 2\right) + \Gamma'\left( \frac 1 2\right)\varepsilon + \frac{\Gamma''(\frac 1 2)}{2}\varepsilon^2 + \cdots\]
        \[\boxed{\therefore \Gamma'\left( \frac 1 2 \right) = -\sqrt{\pi}(\gamma + 2 \ln 2)}\]
        \[\boxed{\therefore \Gamma''\left(\frac 1 2\right)  = \sqrt{\pi}\left[ \frac{\pi^2}{2} + (\gamma + 2 \ln 2)^2\right]}\]
        \item Use the recurrence relation in conjunction with your result from part (c) to determine the exact values of $\Gamma'(\frac 3 2), \Gamma''(\frac 3 2)$
        \\ \\ 
        By the product rule, 
        \[\Gamma'(z+1) = \Gamma(z) + z\Gamma'(z), \Gamma''(z+1) = 2\Gamma'(z) + z\Gamma''(z)\]
        Thus, 
        \[\boxed{\Gamma'\left(\frac 3 2\right) = \sqrt{\pi} \left( 1 - \frac{\gamma}{2} - \ln 2\right)}\]
        \[\boxed{\Gamma''\left( \frac 3 2\right) = \sqrt{\pi} \left[ \frac{\pi^2}{4} + \frac{(\gamma + 2\ln 2)^2}{2} - 2(\gamma + 2 \ln 2)\right]}\]
    \end{enumerate}

    \item Use the duplication identity along with your expression from part (b) of problem 1 and the Beta function integral represenation to determine the exact value of the following integrals:
